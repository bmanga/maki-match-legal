Maki Match Support

We usually reply within two business days: labs.cluno@gmail.com

  • Out of lives: you get a new life every 30 minutes, up to 5. You can also refill them with coins.
  • Coins didn't arrive after a purchase: close the game fully and open it again with an internet connection. It checks for missing purchases when it starts. If they still don't appear, email us.
  • Coin packs don't show in the Shop: check your connection, then tap "Try again".
  • Moving to a new phone: progress and coins are saved on your phone only, so they don't move across and can't be restored.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple: https://reportaproblem.apple.com
  • Start over: on the map, tap the menu button, then Restart all levels. You keep your coins and Chops.
