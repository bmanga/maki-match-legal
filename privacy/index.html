Maki Match Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Maki Match is a puzzle game. We built it to keep your data on your phone.

  • No account. You don't sign up or log in, and we don't ask for your name or email address.
  • Your game stays on your device. Your levels, stars, coins, lives and settings are saved only on your phone.
  • No ads, no tracking. There are no ads and no analytics tools in the game. We don't track you across other apps or websites, and we don't sell or share personal information.
  • Purchases are processed by Apple. We never see or store your payment details. We use RevenueCat to confirm coin purchases: it receives a random ID created by the app (not your name or email), your purchase history for Maki Match, and basic device information such as the iOS version and your country. We use it only to deliver what you bought and to count sales. RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy
  • Children. Maki Match is suitable for all ages. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children, and no account is needed to play.
  • Deleting your data. Settings → Restart all levels clears your level progress. Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your phone. To ask about purchase records held by RevenueCat, email us.

For questions, contact labs.cluno@gmail.com
